# Font credits

Self-hosted here so the quiz works offline and doesn't depend on a
third-party font CDN at runtime.

| File | Font | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fraunces-variable.woff2` | Fraunces | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces |
| `schibstedgrotesk-variable.woff2` | Schibsted Grotesk | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Schibsted+Grotesk |

OFL 1.1 full text: https://openfontlicense.org. Permits embedding,
self-hosting, and commercial use freely.

Downloaded 2026-08-15 directly from Google Fonts' `fonts.gstatic.com` CDN,
then vendored into this repo.

Used in `styles.css`: Fraunces (a warm, soft-contrast serif with an optical
size axis) is the display face for headlines and the big score numerals —
used at large sizes only, for drama at the two "reveal" moments (round
complete, final score). Schibsted Grotesk is the body/UI face for
everything else (questions, answers, labels, buttons).
