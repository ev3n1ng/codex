third-party font CDN at runtime.

self-hosting, and commercial use freely.

Downloaded 2026-08-15 directly from Google Fonts' `fonts.gstatic.com` CDN,
then vendored into this repo.

Used in `styles.css`: Fraunces (a warm, soft-contrast serif with an optical
size axis) is the display face for headlines, the big score numerals, and
the hollow "lineup" numerals/letterforms. Fraunces italic is used for
secondary/editorial text (category descriptions, answer explanations) as a
deliberate second voice, distinct from both the display headlines and the
body UI face. Schibsted Grotesk is the body/UI face for everything else
(questions, answers, labels, buttons).
