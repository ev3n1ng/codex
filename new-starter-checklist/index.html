02

MeWe Daily Post

Post at least once per day on MeWe when there is completed training or a required update to record.

  • Add the required daily update. Keep it brief and factual.
  • Include training where relevant. Only include details that need to be shared.

If MeWe does not open, the fallback will open the MeWe website until a confirmed app deep link is supplied.
